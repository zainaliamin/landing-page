German POS Features

German POS adapts to restaurants, salons, marts, hotels, game zones, and gyms without forcing staff to jump between disconnected tools.

B

Billing and payments

Create sales, apply discounts, handle dues, accept payment methods, and print receipts for different business types.

W

Industry workflows

Use KOT, appointments, barcode checkout, guest billing, session billing, or membership renewals depending on your business.

C

Customers and balances

Keep customer details, phone numbers, addresses, notes, due balances, settlement history, and repeat-visit context.

I

Inventory

Manage stock items, base units, low stock alerts, unit conversions, stock ledgers, and exports.

P

Purchases and suppliers

Record supplier purchases, increase stock automatically, manage supplier profiles, ledgers, and due settlement.

S

Staff and salaries

Manage staff, roles, monthly salary overview, bonuses, deductions, payments, balances, and access controls.

A

Access control

Create cashiers, reset passwords, activate/deactivate users, and control module-level permissions.

T

Tax and receipt settings

Configure payment taxes, prices including tax, receipt content, fiscal details, and country-specific print options.
