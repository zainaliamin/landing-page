How German POS Works

German POS connects sales, payments, inventory, staff control, customers, and reports so owners can manage daily operations from one reliable system.

1

Set up your restaurant

Add menu items, categories, payment methods, tax rules, tables, floors, staff users, receipt content, and delivery sources.

2

Take orders faster

Create dine-in, takeaway, and delivery bills, apply discounts, assign tables, capture customer details, and print receipts or KOTs.

3

Control the kitchen

Send kitchen order tickets with the right item details, notes, table information, waiter, source, and order timing.

4

Track stock and recipes

Map recipes to ingredients, record purchases, monitor low stock, and see the cost price and margin of menu items.

5

Manage cash and users

Configure cashier payment methods, drawer collection, staff access, salaries, due settlements, and shift expenses.

6

Review reports

Use sales, profit and loss, item, shift, expense, inventory, and customer due reports to make better decisions.
