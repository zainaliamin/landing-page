About German POS

About German POS

German POS is designed as separate POS Softwares for businesses that need fast billing, stock or service control, staff access, customer records, payments, and clear owner-level reporting.

Our focus

  • Practical workflows for service, retail, hospitality, and entertainment businesses.
  • Simple screens for cashiers and managers.
  • Connected sales, stock, staff, customers, payments, and profit reports.
  • Support for tax, receipt, and fiscal configuration needs.

Why we built it

Many businesses run billing, inventory, staff, customer records, and reports in separate tools. Each German POS software connects the tools needed for that industry so owners can see what is really happening.

1

For service speed

Cashiers and front desk teams can create bills quickly, apply discounts, select payments, and keep customers moving.

2

For workflow control

Each German POS software is built around its own workflow: kitchen orders, appointments, retail checkout, guest billing, game sessions, or memberships.

3

For owner visibility

Reports connect the sales, tax, expenses, salaries, COGS, stock, and profit or loss details that matter for that business category.

Who it is for

German POS Softwares are suitable for single-outlet businesses and growing multi-branch operations that need category-specific controls.

Restaurants

Dine-in, delivery, KOT, tables, recipes, and item profitability.

Salons

Appointments, services, packages, staff commission, and customer history.

Marts

Fast checkout, inventory, suppliers, purchases, and cashier reports.

Gyms and more

Memberships, sessions, guest billing, dues, attendance, and business reports.
