About German POS

Built to make business operations easier to control.

German POS is designed for businesses that need fast billing, stock or service control, staff access, customer records, payments, and clear owner-level reporting.

Our focus

  • Practical workflows for service, retail, hospitality, and entertainment businesses.
  • Simple screens for cashiers and managers.
  • Connected sales, stock, staff, customers, payments, and profit reports.
  • Support for tax, receipt, and fiscal configuration needs.

Why we built it

Many businesses run billing, inventory, staff, customer records, and reports in separate tools. German POS connects them so owners can see what is really happening.

1

For service speed

Cashiers and front desk teams can create bills quickly, apply discounts, select payments, and keep customers moving.

2

For workflow control

German POS adapts to kitchen orders, appointments, retail checkout, guest billing, game sessions, and memberships.

3

For owner visibility

Reports connect sales, tax, expenses, salaries, COGS, stock, and profit or loss in one place.

Who it is for

German POS is suitable for single-outlet businesses and growing multi-branch operations.

Restaurants

Dine-in, delivery, KOT, tables, recipes, and item profitability.

Salons

Appointments, services, packages, staff commission, and customer history.

Marts

Fast checkout, inventory, suppliers, purchases, and cashier reports.

Gyms and more

Memberships, sessions, guest billing, dues, attendance, and business reports.
