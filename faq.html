German POS FAQs

Answers for business owners comparing POS systems for billing, inventory, staff, customers, tax, and reports.

Which businesses can use German POS?

German POS supports restaurants, salons, marts, hotels, game zones, and gyms with different pages and pricing for each business type.

Can pricing change by country?

Yes. Use the country selector to view relevant package prices for Pakistan, KSA, UAE, Oman, United Kingdom, and USA.

Can I track stock and profit?

Yes. Inventory, purchases, expenses, and reports can help track cost, selling price, and profit depending on the selected business workflow.

Does it support tax settings?

Yes. You can configure tax behavior by payment method, prices including tax, receipt content, and fiscal details where applicable.
