Privacy

Privacy policy.

This page explains how German POS handles inquiry, demo, support, and business contact information submitted through the website.

Information we collect

We may collect restaurant name, contact person, phone number, email address, city, branch count, and product requirements when you request pricing, support, or a demo.

How we use information

Information is used to respond to inquiries, schedule demos, provide support, prepare proposals, improve onboarding, and communicate service updates.

Data sharing

German POS does not sell website inquiry information. Information may be shared only with internal team members or service providers needed to support your request.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@germanpos.com or use the contact form on this website.
